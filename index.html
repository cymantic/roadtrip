THE DRIVE — THERE & BACK

LEG BY LEG

SAT 8AFTER LUNCH · TEA IN CANNES
La Spezia → Cannes → Cassis≈460 km · ~5 h + tea

A12 past Genoa, A10 over the border (expect the Vintimille crawl), then off the A8 at Cannes ~17:00 for tea on the Croisette — an hour of Riviera ritual to break the slog. Back on the A8 past Aix, drop into Cassis for dinner ~20:00.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Tea on the CroisetteThe first holiday frame: cups, palms, and the Carlton's belle-époque façade behind you.
  • Cassis quay, golden hourThe two of you against pastel harbour fronts and bobbing pointus — light lingers past 20:00.
  • Port-Miou calanque pathTen minutes' walk from town: white cliff, pines, moored yachts below. Save it for morning if dinner wins.

Sleep Cassis

Worst traffic of the trip — leave promptly, fuel before Genoa. Vieux Port parking for the tea stop; 45 min is plenty.

SUN 9THE BIG DAY
Cosquer → Arles → horses → Aigues-Mortes → Carcassonne≈330 km · 4½ h driving

Cosquer booked early slot (30 min away) · Arles ~45 min · Route de Cacharel D85A · Aigues-Mortes from ~16:00 · A9/A61 to Carcassonne by early evening.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Espace Van Gogh courtyardStand where he painted it — the garden matches the canvas. Soft shade, good for portraits.
  • Arena arcades, ArlesFrame each other through the Roman arches; lunch shot on Place du Forum.
  • Cacharel white horsesShoot from the car door — horses at the fence line, egrets on their backs. You'll be closest here.
  • Aigues-Mortes from the Salins sideThe money frame: golden ramparts over pink salt water. South/east walls, late-afternoon side light.
  • Rampart walk corridorRepeating arrow slits = portrait tunnel. Tour de Constance for the monumental frame.
  • Carcassonne lit, from Pont VieuxThe classic night two-shot — tripod on the old bridge, citadel glowing behind.

Sleep Carcassonne

Horses doze midday — see them here, shoot the set-piece at Aigues-Mortes. Repellent in the door pocket.

MON 10CAVE & FOOTHILLS
Carcassonne → Mas d'Azil → Pau≈300 km · 3½–4 h

Cité at leisure (lices early, Château Comtal from 10:00), lunch in the Bastide, D119 through the cave, then west along the Pyrenean foothills to Pau for the evening.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Empty lices at 08:30Between the double walls before the coaches — medieval streets to yourselves.
  • THE car shot: Mas d'AzilThe roadster under the cavern arch, river alongside — the trip's signature frame. Hazards on, be quick, drive it twice.
  • Boulevard des Pyrénées at duskPau's kilometre-long terrace with the whole mountain chain across the horizon — tomorrow's route laid out behind you.

Sleep Pau

Feeling strong? Over the Pourtalet tonight and sleep Jaca — doubles tomorrow's slack.

TUE 11THE BIG CROSSING
Pau → Pourtalet → Riglos & Loarre → Zaragoza≈300 km · 4–5 h with stops

D934 up the Ossau valley under the Pic du Midi d'Ossau, over the col at 1,794 m, down the Tena valley. Then two near-route gems: the Mallos de Riglos towers and Castillo de Loarre, via Huesca to Zaragoza. Evening: Aljafería, tapas on Estébanes.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Pic du Midi d'OssauThe Pyrenees' Matterhorn over the D934 — pull-outs above Gabas; morning light is kind.
  • Col du Pourtalet, 1,794 mCar + couple at the frontier marker — the crossing-into-Spain shot, roof down.
  • Mallos de Riglos300 m red conglomerate towers with griffon vultures riding the tops — shoot from the village beneath.
  • Castillo de LoarreRomanesque ramparts on their rock shelf (Kingdom of Heaven's set) — wide from the approach road.
  • Aljafería courtyardInterlaced Moorish arches, golden stone — evening tickets, quiet light.

Sleep Zaragoza area — the crunch booking

Elastic by design: behind, drop Riglos/Loarre and motorway from Sabiñánigo; ahead, linger at both.

WED 12ECLIPSE
Zaragoza → totality → Barcelona outskirts≈310 km · 3 h post-totality

Morning loose (Caesaraugusta museums are cool and air-conditioned). Scout by mid-afternoon — pick two spots. NW Ebro flats for max duration, Parque del Agua for the public event, Jubierre badlands east. Long lunch. Then AP-2 east.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Partials, 19:35→Glasses-on portraits against the lowering sun; solar filter on the a7III for the bitten disc.
  • Totality, 20:29Decide now: one wide frame — corona at 6° over the badlands, you two as silhouettes — then STOP and watch. Don't spend the 85 seconds fiddling.
  • The eclipsed sunset, 21:07Almost nobody stays for it. Silhouette pair, weird light, empty desert.
  • Jubierre hoodoosIf you choose the badlands: ochre towers as the foreground all afternoon.

Sleep 24 h motel, Barcelona outskirts (Martorell / El Prat) — or Lleida at 1 h 45

Ibis Budget / B&B = 24 h kiosk check-in. Expect crowds and possible road controls.

THU 13BARCELONA · PARK & RIDE
Outskirts base → Barcelona by train → back0 driving km — the Mini rests

Slow breakfast at the outskirts base, then leave the car and ride in: from Martorell the FGC runs to Plaça Espanya; from the El Prat side, Rodalies R2 to Passeig de Gràcia. ~30–40 min either way, no city driving, no city parking. Full day in Barcelona — Sagrada Família (book a timed slot, they sell out), the Gothic quarter, vermut, El Rei de la Màgia (Carrer Princesa, trading since 1881) — easy evening train back.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Sagrada Família interiorThe stone forest: late-afternoon sun through the west windows floods the nave red-orange. Gaudí's basilica — finally topped out, 144 years on.
  • Catedral de Santa EulàliaThe actual cathedral, Gothic quarter — cloister with its thirteen geese, quiet after the basilica's crowds.
  • Gothic quarter lanesNarrow stone + hanging lamps — the two of you under the Carrer del Bisbe bridge.
  • Vermut hourCounter portrait at a bodega; the magic shop's 1881 frontage for the card-shop detour.
  • Barceloneta at duskSea end of the day — promenade silhouettes before the train home.

Sleep Same outskirts base as eclipse night — book Wed + Thu together

Two nights, one hotel, zero August Barcelona traffic in an open-top car: the correct choice.

FRI 14COSTA BRAVA
Barcelona → Empúries → Cap de Creus → Cadaqués≈170 km · 2½ h

Ruins mid-morning, swim, then the wild road over the cape — drive it early evening when the campers thin.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Empúries columns + seaGreek stones with the bay behind; swim shot below a 2,500-year-old city.
  • Cap de Creus lighthouse rocksDalí's lunar headland — wind-carved schist, the two of you on the point.
  • Cadaqués eveningWhite lanes, blue boats, church above the bay — promenade at last light.

Sleep Cadaqués — or over the border to Collioure

Ferragosto-eve beds: don't leave this one late.

SAT 15FERRAGOSTO · VENET 15:00
Collioure → Le Muy≈380 km · 4½ h · away by 10:30

Collioure morning, then the A9/A8 haul east; at the Venet Foundation gates for 15:00. Quiet Var evening after.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • The Fauvist frame, CollioureBell tower across the harbour — stand where Matisse and Derain set their easels.
  • Inside Elliptic EclipticLie back, faces + the oval of pure sky — your totality companion piece, four days on.
  • Stella chapel & the corten arcsSculpture park frames everywhere (personal photography — check on entry).

Sleep Le Muy / Les Arcs / Fréjus area — French side tonight

Daytime visit: Skyspace open, light sequence not running — the framed sky is the piece.

SUN 16NO TIMETABLE
Corniche d'Or → Riviera → Balzi Rossi → La Spezia≈330 km · 4 h + stops

D6098 red-rock corniche from the doorstep, long lunch Villefranche or old Antibes, Moyenne/Grande Corniche to Menton. Optional walk-ins: Jardin Exotique (Monaco) or Balzi Rossi (Ventimiglia) + a closing swim. A10/A12 home ~2 h 45.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Corniche d'Or pull-outsRed Esterel porphyry into turquoise — car low in frame, sea behind. Best light before noon.
  • La Turbie Trophy terraceThe high-road view over the whole of Monaco.
  • Baia dei Balzi RossiThe closing swim beneath the Palaeolithic cliff — last frame of the roll.
  • Home: the La Spezia quaySame spot as any departure shot you took — bookends.

Sleep Home — La Spezia

Both caves optional; the road and the sea are the plan.
