THE DRIVE — THERE & BACK

LEG BY LEG

SAT 8TEA IN CANNES · CRÊTES AT DUSK
La Spezia → Cannes → Cassis → Marseille≈490 km · ~5½ h + stops

A12 past Genoa, A10 over the border (expect the Vintimille crawl), off the A8 at Cannes ~17:00 for tea on the Croisette. Then the good bit: leave the motorway at La Ciotat for the Route des Crêtes (D141) over Cap Canaille — the highest sea cliff in France — down into Cassis for golden hour, and on over the Col de la Gineste to Marseille.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Tea on the CroisetteFirst holiday frame: cups, palms, the Carlton's belle-époque façade behind you.
  • Route des Crêtes clifftop394 m of red rock straight down to blue water. Pull-outs all along — car, couple, drop.
  • Cassis harbour, golden hourPastel fronts and pointus below Cap Canaille. Light lingers past 20:00.
  • Cité Radieuse rooftopLe Corbusier's sculptural roof terrace at dusk — brutalist geometry, MAMO art centre, the sea beyond.

Sleep Hotel Le Corbusier, Marseille — inside the Cité Radieuse (UNESCO)

Arrive with light if you can: the rooftop is the reason to be here. Crêtes road closes in high fire-risk wind — check before turning off.

SUN 9THE BIG DAY
Marseille → Cosquer → Arles → horses → Aigues-Mortes → Carcassonne≈330 km · 4½ h driving

Cosquer booked early slot (20 min from the hotel) · Arles ~45 min · Route de Cacharel D85A · Aigues-Mortes from ~16:00 · A9/A61 to Carcassonne by early evening.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Espace Van Gogh courtyardStand where he painted it — the garden matches the canvas. Soft shade, good for portraits.
  • Arena arcades, ArlesFrame each other through the Roman arches; lunch shot on Place du Forum.
  • Cacharel white horsesShoot from the car door — horses at the fence line, egrets on their backs. You'll be closest here.
  • Aigues-Mortes from the Salins sideThe money frame: golden ramparts over pink salt water. South/east walls, late-afternoon side light.
  • Rampart walk corridorRepeating arrow slits = portrait tunnel. Tour de Constance for the monumental frame.
  • Carcassonne lit, from Pont VieuxThe classic night two-shot — tripod on the old bridge, citadel glowing behind.

Sleep Carcassonne — the Bastide

Horses doze midday — see them here, shoot the set-piece at Aigues-Mortes. Repellent in the door pocket.

MON 10CAVE & FOOTHILLS
Carcassonne → Mas d'Azil → Pau → Oloron≈340 km · 4 h

Cité at leisure (lices early, Château Comtal from 10:00), lunch in the Bastide, D119 through the cave, then west along the foothills. Pull into Pau late afternoon for the Boulevard des Pyrénées view and a coffee, then the short hop south to Oloron.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Empty lices at 08:30Between the double walls before the coaches — medieval streets to yourselves.
  • THE car shot: Mas d'AzilThe roadster under the cavern arch, river alongside — the trip's signature frame. Hazards on, be quick, drive it twice.
  • Boulevard des Pyrénées, PauKilometre-long terrace with the whole chain across the horizon — tomorrow's route laid out in front of you. Drive-by stop, coffee, back on the road.

Sleep Hôtel de la Paix, Oloron-Sainte-Marie

Oloron puts you in the foothills: Tuesday starts with one turn onto the valley road, no city extraction.

TUE 11THE BIG CROSSING
Oloron → Pourtalet → Riglos & Loarre → Zaragoza≈290 km · 4–5 h with stops

Ten minutes on the way out: the Romanesque portal of Sainte-Marie — carved marble, 12th century, UNESCO-listed, with wine-harvest and salmon-fishing scenes in the arch. Then the D934 up the Ossau valley under the Pic du Midi d'Ossau, over the col at 1,794 m, down the Tena valley. Then two near-route gems: the Mallos de Riglos towers and Castillo de Loarre, via Huesca to Zaragoza. Evening: Aljafería, tapas on Estébanes.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Sainte-Marie's portal, OloronTwelfth-century carved marble — the chained figures holding up the arch, salmon and grape harvest in the voussoirs.
  • Pic du Midi d'OssauThe Pyrenees' Matterhorn over the D934 — pull-outs above Gabas; morning light is kind.
  • Col du Pourtalet, 1,794 mCar + couple at the frontier marker — the crossing-into-Spain shot, roof down.
  • Mallos de Riglos300 m red conglomerate towers with griffon vultures riding the tops — shoot from the village beneath.
  • Castillo de LoarreRomanesque ramparts on their rock shelf (Kingdom of Heaven's set) — wide from the approach road.
  • Aljafería courtyardInterlaced Moorish arches, golden stone — evening tickets, quiet light.

Sleep Hotel & Spa Real Ciudad de Zaragoza

Elastic by design: behind, drop Riglos/Loarre and motorway from Sabiñánigo; ahead, linger at both.

WED 12ECLIPSE
Zaragoza → totality → Sant Andreu≈310 km · 3 h post-totality

Morning loose (Caesaraugusta museums are cool and air-conditioned). Scout by mid-afternoon — pick two spots. NW Ebro flats for max duration, Parque del Agua for the public event, Jubierre badlands east. Long lunch. Then AP-2 east.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Partials, 19:35→Glasses-on portraits against the lowering sun; solar filter on the a7III for the bitten disc.
  • Totality, 20:29Decide now: one wide frame — corona at 6° over the badlands, you two as silhouettes — then STOP and watch. Don't spend the 85 seconds fiddling.
  • The eclipsed sunset, 21:07Almost nobody stays for it. Silhouette pair, weird light, empty desert.
  • Jubierre hoodoosIf you choose the badlands: ochre towers as the foreground all afternoon.

Sleep Ibis Barcelona Sant Andreu (night 1 of 2)

Ring-road loop at 1am is empty and fine. Expect crowds and possible road controls near popular viewing sites.

THU 13MOUNTAIN · CRYPT · CITY
Sant Andreu → Montserrat → Colònia Güell → Barcelona by metro≈120 km driving + L1 into town

07:30 away · 08:30 Montserrat, ahead of the coaches — drive the BP-1103 switchbacks up from Monistrol and skip both the rack railway and the cable car queues · 12:30 lunch · 14:30 Colònia Güell (free parking, allow 2 h) · 17:00 park the car at the hotel for the night · 18:15 metro L1 direct into town, ~20 min · late Barcelona evening, dinner at 21:00 like a local.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Montserrat pillars at 09:00Eroded conglomerate fingers with morning shadow still in them — Sant Joan funicular for the high view.
  • Santa Cova pathThe grotto funicular and the modernista rosary monuments (Gaudí among the architects) — cave, pilgrimage and sculpture in one walk.
  • Gaudí's crypt, Colònia GüellLeaning basalt columns and brick vault — his structural laboratory. Inside: the upside-down hanging-chain model that solved the Sagrada's statics.
  • Gothic quarter after darkNarrow stone + hanging lamps — the two of you under the Carrer del Bisbe bridge, no crowds by then.
  • Santa Maria del MarThe cathedral of the sea, El Born — then vermut and a 21:00 table.

Sleep Ibis Barcelona Sant Andreu (night 2 of 2)

Montserrat first: Colònia Güell is on the road back down, and the monastery fills with coaches by 11:00. Choir is on summer break — no Escolania. Check the last L1 home before you settle into dinner. Flattened by the eclipse night? Drop Montserrat; the crypt and the city evening stand alone.

FRI 14COSTA BRAVA
Sant Andreu → Empúries → Cap de Creus → Cadaqués → Portbou≈200 km · 3 h

Ruins mid-morning, swim, then the wild road over the cape — drive it early evening when the campers thin — and on up the coast to Portbou for the night.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Empúries columns + seaGreek stones with the bay behind; swim shot below a 2,500-year-old city.
  • Cap de Creus lighthouse rocksDalí's lunar headland — wind-carved schist, the two of you on the point.
  • Cadaqués eveningWhite lanes, blue boats, church above the bay — promenade at last light.
  • Passages, PortbouDani Karavan's Walter Benjamin memorial: a rusted steel corridor driven down through the cliff to a glass pane above the churning sea. Go at last light.

Sleep Hotel Comodoro, Portbou

Benjamin died here in 1940 fleeing the Gestapo; the memorial and his grave are a short walk from the hotel. Sets up a beautiful Saturday start.

SAT 15FERRAGOSTO · VENET 15:00
Portbou → Collioure → Le Muy≈400 km · 5 h · away by 09:30

Start on the D914 over the Coll dels Belitres — 30 km of vineyard terraces above the sea, Spain to France the slow way — into Collioure for late breakfast. Then the A9/A8 haul east; at the Venet Foundation gates for 15:00.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Coll dels Belitres descentTerraced Banyuls vineyards dropping to the Mediterranean — the border crossing worth doing at 30 mph.
  • The Fauvist frame, CollioureBell tower across the harbour — stand where Matisse and Derain set their easels.
  • Inside Elliptic EclipticLie back, faces + the oval of pure sky — your totality companion piece, four days on.
  • Stella chapel & the corten arcsSculpture park frames everywhere (personal photography — check on entry).

Sleep Le Château d'Argens, Le Muy

Daytime visit: Skyspace open, light sequence not running — the framed sky is the piece.

SUN 16NO TIMETABLE
Corniche d'Or → Riviera → Balzi Rossi → La Spezia≈330 km · 4 h + stops

D6098 red-rock corniche from the doorstep, long lunch Villefranche or old Antibes, Moyenne/Grande Corniche to Menton. Optional walk-ins: Jardin Exotique (Monaco) or Balzi Rossi (Ventimiglia) + a closing swim. A10/A12 home ~2 h 45.

Photo ops — the two of you

  • Corniche d'Or pull-outsRed Esterel porphyry into turquoise — car low in frame, sea behind. Best light before noon.
  • La Turbie Trophy terraceThe high-road view over the whole of Monaco.
  • Baia dei Balzi RossiThe closing swim beneath the Palaeolithic cliff — last frame of the roll.
  • Home: the La Spezia quaySame spot as any departure shot you took — bookends.

Sleep Home — La Spezia

Both caves optional; the road and the sea are the plan.
